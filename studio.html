The site studio

The toolkit on paper, made workable on screen. Describe a place — by drawing its plan or by answering the assessment — and the studio reads back its publicness, its interaction, the uses it could hold, the interventions that fit, and how each one tends to be funded. Swipe the deck, drag interventions onto the plan, rewrite the week, then take the brief with you. Nothing is stored or sent.

The publicness diagnostic

Score your site as it behaves on an ordinary Tuesday — not as its policy claims. Slide each dimension from closed (0) to fully open (5).

Draw the plot

Sketch your site as simple rooms and grounds: drag on the plan to draw a zone, or add one with a button and move it into place. Find your site on the map first and the plan is drawn over the real thing — zones stay pinned to the ground as you pan and zoom, and the studio reads their true size.
