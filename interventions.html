Before the doors open

    Finding the groups who might come

    Step two is the one most groups skip. Three ways to do it with this site:

    1. The opportunities map — which nearby sites carry which uses, and therefore which local needs are unmet.
    2. The case studies — what has worked in buildings like yours. Search the same use locally and you will usually find a group in a worse room.
    3. The site studio — ranks the groups most compatible with your site, and names what each would need before saying yes.

    Offline: your borough's community directory, the council for voluntary service, the library noticeboard, and the local social media group.

    What decides whether it works

    Four years of research across community-led projects in historic places of worship found the same four things separating projects that reached their potential from those that stalled. All four are about people, not fabric.
